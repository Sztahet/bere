# Vaelis i Azmir — prompty portretowe

Oba portrety przedstawiają bohaterów w 12 ET. Wygląd, kolory oczu, fryzury i kostiumy są propozycjami do oceny, a nie zatwierdzonymi elementami kanonu. Do każdego promptu warto dołączyć te same portrety bohaterów Riftorynu jako referencje stylu. Do modelu można później wykonać osobny arkusz całej sylwetki z przodu, profilu i tyłu na podstawie wybranego portretu.

## Vaelis — młoda magini Ciemności

> Polished painterly fantasy game character portrait for Riftoryn, portrait orientation, 4:5, waist-up, a 23-year-old female Darkness specialist mage and apprentice archivist from the Shadow Port of Vesper. Intelligent, observant, independently minded, with a thoughtful expression and the confidence to question her teacher; warm and human, not sinister. Deep brown hair with a subtle cool-plum sheen, arranged in a practical asymmetrical braid with a few loose strands; grey-green eyes, natural features and a believable young adult face. Practical layered archivist's clothing: charcoal and muted indigo fitted coat, soft dark-violet lining, weathered leather document strap, restrained silver fastenings and functional sleeves, no heavy armor. She holds a small closed field notebook in one hand; with the other she carefully shapes a thin veil of translucent violet shadow over the notebook to shield it from rain. Subtle magic with a clear limit, not a spell explosion. Silhouette and face unobstructed, identifiable hands, detailed cloth and leather, cool light, transparent background, no environment behind her. Cohesive character-select portrait in the same painterly fantasy game-art style as the provided Riftoryn references; distinct from Azmir and Morrak. No text, letters, logos, watermark, extra fingers or limbs, skull decorations, revealing costume, photorealism, or comic panels.

## Azmir — archiwista z Cienistego Portu

> Polished painterly fantasy game character portrait for Riftoryn, portrait orientation, 4:5, waist-up, a 68-year-old male Darkness specialist mage and senior archivist from the Shadow Port of Vesper. An older man with a convincingly aged face, silver-grey hair brushed back, a short neatly kept grey beard, thoughtful dark brown eyes and a patient, searching expression; quiet authority, with a trace of weariness and dry humor. He is a practical scholar and protector, not a villain or a king. Layered charcoal and muted plum robes under a weathered dark coat, functional sleeves, restrained silver clasps, a simple leather folio strap; no ornate armor or crown. One hand rests on a closed archival folio; above it the other holds a small, controlled veil of soft violet shadow, sheltering the folio from a narrow beam of daylight. Subtle magic, clearly visible hands, face unobstructed, distinctive readable silhouette, aged skin and detailed cloth, warm light balanced with cool violet shadows, transparent background, no environment behind him. Cohesive character-select portrait in the same painterly fantasy game-art style as the provided Riftoryn references; clearly older and visually distinct from Vaelis and Morrak. No text, letters, logos, watermark, extra fingers or limbs, skull decorations, exaggerated spell effects, photorealism, or comic panels.
