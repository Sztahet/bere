# Wozy Riftorynu — prompty do arkuszy modelowania

Sześć propozycji wizualnych do przyszłego modelowania, a nie zatwierdzone modele 3D ani nowe zasady świata. Każdy prompt generuj osobno. Wóz Vedy opiera się na opowiadaniach: konie, skrzynie, burty, pasy mocujące i łatana plandeka są w nich obecne. Pozostałe szczegóły konstrukcyjne wszystkich wozów to propozycje.

Dostarczone plansze: [Veda](assets/lore/wagons/veda.png), [Verdan](assets/lore/wagons/verdan.png), [Kharum](assets/lore/wagons/kharum.png), [Vareth](assets/lore/wagons/vareth.png), [Nivoren](assets/lore/wagons/nivoren.png) i [Vesper](assets/lore/wagons/vesper.png). Ich opisy i powiększane ilustracje znajdują się w kodeksie Unity: **Życie świata → Wozy**.

Dodatkowe warianty: [kryty wóz przewoźnika](assets/lore/wagons/kryty-przewoznika.png) jako referencja dla wozu Vedy i podobnych przewozów oraz [rozkładany warsztat](assets/lore/wagons/warsztat-rozkladany.png). W kodeksie są dostępne jako drugi wariant odpowiednio przy Vedzie i Vareth. To ilustracyjne referencje, nie gotowe modele 3D.

## Wspólne ustawienia

Do każdego promptu dołącz poniższy fragment. Jeśli generator umożliwia użycie referencji, można dodać [mapy Riftorynu](assets/lore/cartography/) jako wskazówkę nastroju i położenia krain. Konie, jeżeli są pokazane, mają być osobno od widoków samego wozu, aby nie zasłaniać konstrukcji.

> Stylized high-end fantasy game vehicle production sheet for Riftoryn, landscape 3:2. One coherent, practical horse-drawn wagon design, unhitched and unloaded in the main turnaround. Clear separate front, side, rear and 3/4 views of the same wagon with consistent wheel count, dimensions, axle placement, body, hitch and construction. Large readable detail views of wheels, axle, hitch, cargo ties and characteristic equipment; separate small loaded view showing intended use. Show realistic structural support, clearance and attachment points suitable as an artist's reference for a future stylized 3D model. Handcrafted wood, iron and textiles with believable wear; readable silhouette, warm painterly materials, restrained geometric shapes. Neutral simple background, even studio lighting. No text, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, modern tires, engine, exhaust, floating parts or impossible wheel geometry. Do not treat visual details as established lore or finalized engineering.

## 1. Wóz Vedy — przewoźniczka z Kharum

> A working long-distance freight wagon belonging to Veda, a human carrier from Kharum. Four wooden spoked wheels with iron rims, sturdy timber frame, practical driver's bench for two, useful sideboards and a rear loading area. A weathered, visibly patched canvas tarpaulin protects a mixed load of secured wooden crates and parcels; show removable cargo and accessible straps in the separate loaded view. Add a spare tie-down strap and modest repair tools, with no elaborate ornament. Suggest many journeys through dust and repaired canvas rather than neglect. Hitch suitable for a pair of horses, horses shown separately only as a scale reference. Keep the tarp and cargo removable so the structure underneath can be modeled.

**Uwaga:** zadbaj, by skrzynię można było przesunąć, a pas i plandekę odpiąć, jak w historii. Nie pokazuj zawartości zamkniętej paczki jako ustalonej.

## 2. Wóz rolniczy Verdan

> A modest agricultural wagon used by farming communities of Verdan. Four wooden spoked wheels, simple sturdy undercarriage, low removable sideboards and a wide open bed that can be loaded by hand. Restrained green and warm natural wood accents, worn but cared for, rope ties and practical repairs. Separate cargo view with baskets, grain sacks, produce and seed boxes, secured so nothing floats. Horse-drawn hitch, no horses obscuring the views. Ordinary working equipment, not a ceremonial carriage; no plants growing out of the wagon and no magical crop production.

## 3. Ciężki wóz Kharum

> A heavy freight wagon for stone blocks and ore from Kharum. Four thick wooden spoked wheels with broad iron rims, low reinforced bed, substantial axles and visible load-bearing timber beams. Practical braces, tie-down points, removable chocks and a sturdy hitch for draft horses. In a separate loaded view, show a modest, believable load of stone and ore secured with chains and ropes; keep it low enough not to overturn the wagon. Dark timber, iron and mineral dust, repaired fittings. No giant decorative armor, tank tracks, powered wheels or unsupported loads.

## 4. Wóz warsztatowy Vareth

> A traveling repair workshop wagon associated with Vareth. Four wooden spoked wheels, a stable wagon bed with a fold-out work surface that has visible hinges and supports, lockable tool drawers, spare brackets and a compact manual vise. Show the wagon closed in the main views and the supported workspace opened in a separate detail view. Muted warm red and dark wood, soot and traces of repair; no open furnace while traveling. An optional small safely housed splotyt work light may be shown as a separate accessory, without assuming that it powers movement or creates energy. Horse-drawn, practical and serviceable, not a siege engine.

## 5. Wóz górski Nivoren

> A compact mountain supply wagon for the roads of Nivoren, carrying food, medicines and replacement parts. Four wooden spoked wheels, a narrow but stable body, sturdy hitch, weather-resistant canvas cover, closed strapped supply chests and a compartment for blankets and road tools. Restrained blue-grey fabric, worn pale wood and modest frost on exposed surfaces. Show optional removable wheel-grip fittings separately as an unconfirmed concept, not an established invention. The main vehicle remains a wheeled horse-drawn wagon, with no permanently attached skis, engines or magical propulsion.

## 6. Wóz konwojowy Vesper

> An ordinary-looking covered freight wagon used by a Vesper convoy. Four wooden spoked wheels, muted dark wood, understated removable canvas cover, secure accessible crates, practical tie-downs and a driver's bench. Design it for discreet transport rather than a sinister or armored appearance; avoid skulls, spikes, prison bars and overt criminal symbols. In separate detail views show ordinary load securing, a protected document pouch and an optional covered crystal container; do not depict the container as proof of an established masking device. Horse-drawn, with no visible magic required for movement.

## Kontrola po wygenerowaniu

Sprawdź tę samą liczbę kół i zgodny układ w każdym widoku, czytelne mocowanie osi i dyszla, możliwość załadunku oraz to, czy detale da się wykonać jako osobne elementy. Rozmiary, liczba koni, rodzaje zabezpieczeń i ostateczne materiały wymagają decyzji przed modelowaniem. Obrazek jest referencją, nie gotowym modelem 3D.
