# Nera — prompt portretu

Propozycja wyglądu do oceny: Nera ma około 21 lat w 12 ET, jest młodą dorosłą z Zerath i uczy się pracy przy przekaźnikach oraz magii Błyskawic. Ma zostać bohaterką Wsparcia. Fryzura, kolory i szczegóły stroju z promptu nie są jeszcze zatwierdzonym kanonem; jej umiejętności w grze pozostają do opracowania.

**Prompt:**

> Polished stylized fantasy game character portrait for Riftoryn, vertical 4:5 portrait, waist-up, a 21-year-old adult female Lightning support hero from Zerath, a young relay-station apprentice learning from experienced technicians and couriers. Warm, open expression with lively curiosity and a hint of determination, approachable and capable, not a child. Distinctive chestnut-brown hair in a practical loose side braid with a few flyaway strands, amber-brown eyes, lightly sun-warmed skin; expressive face and a clear, recognizable silhouette. Practical layered indigo and muted blue work clothes with small golden-yellow lightning accents, worn leather utility straps, fingerless work gloves and a compact satchel holding a notebook and a few simple hand tools. She holds a small protected signal lamp containing a crystal, with a faint controlled blue-white glow suggesting lightning energy stored inside; a couple of delicate sparks nearby, subtle rather than explosive. Her outfit suggests someone learning on the job: resourceful, ready to help and still developing her own confidence, with no crown or ornate heavy armor. Rich shapes, appealing stylized proportions, crisp readable face, painterly high-end fantasy game illustration, polished materials, soft cinematic lighting, vibrant indigo, warm amber and electric-blue accents. Clean transparent background, single character, portrait composition matching the attached Riftoryn hero portraits. Give her a distinct design: do not copy Varek's white hair, large yellow lightning collar or face markings. No other characters, no text, letters, logos, watermark, photorealism, giant weapons, excessive lightning, extra limbs or cluttered background.

Traktuj je jako odniesienie do stylu i kolorystyki Zerath, nie jako wzór wyglądu Nery. Ten prompt nie przesądza jej zdolności bojowych ani ostatecznego wyglądu.
